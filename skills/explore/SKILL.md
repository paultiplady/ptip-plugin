---
name: explore
description: Create and maintain a living exploration document for open-ended research, taste mapping, recommendations, or curation. Use when the user says explore, curate, map a space, continue a discovery thread, or capture iterative findings and preferences.
---

Iteratively explore a domain and produce a living document containing a knowledge map. Engage the user with curiosity and probe their interests to calibrate your search, aiming for 3-5 data points per round of feedback.  

1. Read the project and Personal Context. Resolve the canonical document system (e.g. Google Docs, Notion) from memory; if absent, ask once and store the answer in memory.
2. Maintain three top-level pages/tabs:
   - **Summary:** human-readable map, conclusions, and shortlist.
   - **AI Prompt:** self-contained objective, anchors/anti-anchors, evidence, frontier hypotheses, unknowns, and next probes.
   - **Results:** brief index plus one nested page per topic with findings, links/media, user reaction, and implication.
3. Human feedback is gold; carefully preserve the provenance and distinguish from your own inferences. Where possible cite the feedback’s specific message/session for future reference. Consider explore/exploit ratio and discuss with the user to balance according to their preference.
4. Keep the Summary terse, operating state in AI Prompt, and bulk detail in topic pages. Use nesting and hyperlinking for progressive disclosure to keep each page from growing too large.
